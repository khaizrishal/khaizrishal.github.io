## 🛠️ Setting Up Your First Library

### 📚 Connecting Goodreads

1. Open dewarr after installation
2. Navigate to the Settings section
3. Select "Goodreads" from the integrations menu
4. Click "Connect Account"
5. Follow the prompts to authorize dewarr to access your Goodreads data
6. Your existing shelves and ratings will sync automatically

### 📚 Connecting Hardcover

1. In Settings, choose "Hardcover"
2. Click "Link Account"
3. Enter your Hardcover credentials
4. Confirm the connection
5. Your Hardcover activity will now appear in dewarr

### 🔊 Connecting Audiobookshelf

1. In Settings, find "Audiobookshelf"
2. Enter your Audiobookshelf server address
3. Provide your API key or authentication details
4. Select which libraries you want dewarr to manage
5. Test the connection to ensure everything works
