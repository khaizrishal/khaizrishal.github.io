title: "The Prompt Recipe"
weight: 2
---

A reusable prompt template for getting clearer, safer answers from any chatbot.

> Explain **[topic]** in plain language for a beginner. Give me three key points,
> one everyday example and three questions I can use to check the information
> with an official source. If a fact may have changed recently, say that clearly.

Then check the sources yourself before you act on the answer.
