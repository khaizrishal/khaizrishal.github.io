**Simply Learn AI** is a plain-language guide to using artificial intelligence
with confidence and caution.

You do not need to be a technology expert to use AI wisely. With a little
curiosity and common sense you can get real value from these tools while
protecting yourself and the people around you.

### What you will find here

* **Understand** what AI can do and where it falls short.
* **Spot hallucinations** and tell useful information from confident fiction.
* **Protect your privacy** and know what to keep out of a chatbot.
* **Recognise scams and fakes**, including cloned voices and deepfakes.
* **Check before you act** whenever health, money or safety is at stake.
